The Thread

Two of you, tied together in the dark, on a line.
You will not be able to see each other. You will feel each other through the thread, and hear each other, and that is all.

Nothing runs in between. To be joined you must pass a piece of text back and forth once, by whatever way you already talk.

Send this to them.

They will send one back. Put it here.

Put theirs here.

Send this back to them, and wait.
