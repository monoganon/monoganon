Ascend

Two of you, roped together, at the bottom of something tall.
You will not see each other. You will feel each other on the rope, and hear each other, and that is all there is.

Whoever is still can see. Whoever is moving cannot. So one of you holds and looks and talks, and the other one climbs blind, and then you swap, and you keep swapping until you are at the top or she has you.

Nothing runs in between. To be tied together you must pass a piece of text back and forth once, by whatever way you already talk.

Send this to them.

This contains your network address. Send it the way you would send a phone number — to them, not to a room.

They will send one back. Put it here.

Put theirs here.

Send this back to them, and wait.

This contains your network address too.
